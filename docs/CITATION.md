# How to cite ActinoScope

**Software author:** Imene Maallem.

**Research affiliation:** Floret Center for Advanced Genomics and Bioinformatics Research, Nigeria. This identifies the author's stated research affiliation and does **not** imply the Center owns, sponsors, endorses, or has approved distribution of ActinoScope.

**Release status:** Beta 1 is being tested; no official public software release or DOI has yet been published.

The citation metadata is recorded in [CITATION.cff](../CITATION.cff). An approved public release will add the exact software version, publication year, and the actual version-specific Zenodo DOI.

A future complete citation will look like:

> Maallem, I. (year). *ActinoScope: From bacterial genomes to enzymes and natural products* (version). [Computer software]. Zenodo. DOI to be assigned.

When reporting research results, cite the exact version of ActinoScope and the underlying scientific tools and reference databases actually used, such as Bakta and antiSMASH. Computational predictions are not experimental confirmation.

**Copyright and distribution rights have not yet been established.** Institutional affiliation alone does not settle ownership of software or permission to distribute it under the Center's name.
